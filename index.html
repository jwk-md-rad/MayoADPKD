Measurement Instructions

Measurements are taken on MRI (preferred) or CT. Use the largest kidney dimension on each plane. All measurements in centimetres (cm).

  1. Sagittal plane (A) — on the parasagittal slice showing the maximum longitudinal kidney diameter, measure:
    • Ls — cranio-caudal length: longest pole-to-pole distance
  2. Coronal plane (B) — on the coronal slice showing the maximum longitudinal kidney diameter, measure:
    • Lc — cranio-caudal length: longest pole-to-pole distance
  3. Axial / Transverse plane (C) — on the axial slice at the maximum transverse diameter (perpendicular to the longitudinal axis), measure 2 dimensions:
    • W — widest lateral (transverse) diameter
    • D — antero-posterior depth, perpendicular to W
  4. Total: 4 measurements per kidney. The ellipsoid formula averages both length measurements:
    L = (Lc + Ls) / 2
    Volume (mL) = (π/6) × L × W × D
  5. Enter the patient's height in cm to compute htTKV = TKV / height (mL/m), which determines the Mayo class.

Suppl. Figure S2 — Estimation of TKV using the ellipsoid equation (Irazabal et al. JASN 2015)

Length was obtained from longitudinal images at the maximum longitudinal diameter, in both sagittal (A) and coronal (B) planes. Width and depth were obtained from transverse images (C) at the maximum transverse diameter, acquired in the plane perpendicular to the longitudinal plane.
